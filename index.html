AM²

Advanced Materials and Mechanics Research Lab

We model and engineer the mechanics of energy-storage materials, from the interfaces inside solid-state cells to load-bearing batteries and nanocomposites.

Solid-state battery interface modeling

Multiscale models of how interfaces deform, delaminate and fail inside solid-state cells.

Structural batteries

Energy storage that also carries mechanical load, designed as part of the structure itself.

Metallic and polymer nanocomposites

Nano-reinforced metal and polymer matrices, tuned for strength, stiffness and function.

What we bring

Cohesive-zone and finite-element modeling, nanoindentation and nanoscratch characterization, battery pack design and manufacturing experience.
